Website Planning Document: GreenThumb Gardening

Site Name

GreenThumb Gardening

This name was chosen to represent a website focused on helping urban gardeners grow healthier and more sustainable gardens. "GreenThumb" is a popular phrase that connects with gardening enthusiasts and implies expertise and success in plant care.

Optional domain availability: greenthumbgardens.org

Site Purpose

The site will serve as a go-to resource for urban gardeners, offering tips, tutorials, downloadable guides, and community forums. It will also feature seasonal planting calendars and product recommendations for indoor and outdoor gardening.

Scenarios

  • How can I start a container garden on my apartment balcony?
  • What plants are best suited for small indoor spaces with limited sunlight?

Color Scheme

  • #2e7d32 (Deep Green) – Used for headings and key accent elements.
  • #f3fff3 (Soft Green) – Used as the site’s background color for a fresh, organic look.

Typography

  • Georgia, serif – Used for the main site title and page headings to provide a touch of elegance and authority.
  • Segoe UI, sans-serif – Used for body text and navigation for readability and a clean, modern appearance.
